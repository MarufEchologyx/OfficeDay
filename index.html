OfficeDay · food and transport for every working day

One app replaces the WhatsApp car groups and the daily food polls. Employees confirm the next working day once; lunch, evening snacks and a seat in their car follow. Drivers get each trip's route built from who confirmed, and admins get exact meal counts, a live map and every car's daily kilometres. Every picture is a screenshot of the working prototype in light and dark. Use the Screen explorer to open any screen and see what every option on it leads to, and Flows to follow each journey step by step. Names, phones, places and counts are example data.

How it works

Today it runs on WhatsApp: one group per car, three branch groups with food polls, and someone counting votes by hand. OfficeDay keeps the same habits and removes the counting.

Principles
  • A default that fits each personAt setup people choose "mostly office" (every working day is coming) or "mostly remote" (only their usual office days). They act only when plans change.
  • One decision per dayAttendance and branch drive food and transport. Each service can still be switched off on its own.
  • Cut-offs are visibleEvery screen says when a choice locks. After the lock, changes go to an admin as a request.
  • Works without the driverIf the driver isn't on the app, the first rider in the car starts the trip and shares the car's location.
  • Clean main screenToday shows only coming, food, car and a report icon. Branch, pickup point and skipping a ride sit under More options.
  • Push onlyNo SMS and no email. Every message is a push notification and is also kept in the in-app inbox.
Who uses it
  • EmployeeSigns in with Microsoft (company tenant). Confirms days, looks back at past days (read only), plans up to 30 days, works from another branch, picks another car and a pickup on its paths, follows the car live, calls car members, starts the trip, and reports problems from inside the car.
  • Driver (optional)Phone number and a 6-digit PIN. Forgot PIN: a reset code goes to their phone by SMS (the only SMS the app sends), then they type a new PIN twice. An admin can also set a PIN for drivers who struggle with phones. Same design system as employees with bigger type and three tabs: Trips, History, Me. Reports from the active trip.
  • Branch adminSame app, branch screens. Today shows the three numbers that matter: people in office, total lunch, total snacks, for any day (past days read only). Who's in with phone numbers and a call button, food split by diet (normal, vegetarian, vegan, no beef), add guests, and cars arriving. No vendor connection: food is ordered by hand from these counts.
  • Roles and peopleEveryone comes from the HR employee API and starts as an Employee. People who aren't in HR (like some drivers) are added by hand. Any admin can give anyone more roles, make someone an admin, or change a role. Someone with two roles chooses one after sign-in and can switch from the avatar. Forgot PIN and forgot password are the same steps for everyone registered: a code by SMS or email, then the new one typed twice; an admin can also set one.
  • Transport and super adminWeb admin, Microsoft sign-in. Cars, drivers and members, the live map, routes, reports and traffic, mileage, calendar, rules and integrations.

Who uses it and roles · 6
  • One app, four rolesEmployees, drivers and branch admins use one iOS and Android app. Transport and super admins use the web admin. Each role sees its own screens in the same design.
  • Everyone starts from HRAll people come from the HR employee API and start as Employee. Someone not in HR (for example a driver) is added by hand by an admin.
  • Admins manage rolesAny admin can give anyone more roles, change any role, or make someone an admin. Every change is written to the audit log.
  • Two or more rolesAfter sign-in the person picks which role to use and can tick Remember my choice. They switch role any time from the avatar menu.
  • Avatar on every screenEvery screen of every role has the avatar top right. It shows who is signed in and their role, and opens the account menu (profile, theme, role switch, sign out).
  • No separate trip captainThere is no special captain role. Any rider in a car can start the trip or report a problem.
Sign-in · 11
  • One sign-in screenThe same screen for all roles. Microsoft (company tenant only) is the default button. Phone number and Email sit underneath. No badges or highlight boxes.
  • MicrosoftOnly accounts in the company Microsoft tenant can sign in. Personal or other organisations' accounts are blocked with a clear message.
  • No 'signed in' screenAfter a successful sign-in the app moves on by itself: to setup the first time, or straight to Today if setup is done.
  • Email or phone: step 1The person types their email or phone and taps Next. The app checks whether they are registered (HR or added by an admin).
  • Email or phone: step 2If a password or PIN already exists, its field appears with Forgot and 'Use PIN / password instead'. If none exists, a one-time code is sent. If not registered, the app says so.
  • First-time password or PINThe one-time code goes to that email or phone and works once, for 10 minutes. Then the person sets a password, a 6-digit PIN, or both.
  • Last used option firstThe next time, the sign-in option used last opens first, marked Last used.
  • 6-digit PINPINs are always 6 digits and typed twice when set. Simple PINs like 123456 or the phone number are not allowed.
  • Forgot PIN or passwordThe same steps for everyone: code by SMS or email, then the new one typed twice, then back to sign in. Only registered people can reset.
  • Admin can set itAn admin can set a PIN or password for anyone, for example drivers who struggle with phones. Admins never see the PIN itself.
  • Web sessionThe web admin signs out after 30 minutes idle. Saved work is never lost.
First-time setup · 4
  • Step 1: permissionsLocation and notification permission, each explained before the phone's own dialog.
  • Step 2: how you workMostly office means every working day is 'coming'. Mostly remote means only the chosen office days. This is the default plan.
  • Step 3: pickup pointThe usual pickup point is set on the map along the car's route, and the person agrees to share their phone number with their car.
  • Setup only onceAfter setup the person never sees it again. Everything can be changed later from Me.
Planning days · 7
  • Coming by defaultDays follow the default plan. The person only acts when a plan changes.
  • Plan up to 30 daysMany days can be marked at once (coming, not coming, other branch).
  • Leave always winsThe leave API marks the day 'not coming' automatically and overrides any plan. If leave is cancelled, the plan comes back.
  • Holidays and weekendsHolidays sync from the holidays app. Weekends and special working days or days off are set by admins.
  • Past days read onlyPast days can be opened from the week strip to see what happened (food served, ride times, route). Nothing can be changed.
  • Weekend and holiday screensToday shows a clear weekend or holiday card and the next working day with its food and car.
  • No requests or approvalsNobody asks an admin for permission. Changes before the deadline just happen; after it the counts are final.
Food · 7

  • Food deadlineAdmin sets it per branch: hours before the meal or a fixed time the day before (for example 8:00 pm). A reminder goes out before it.
  • After the deadlineFood counts lock and are final. A ride can still be skipped and the car seat is released automatically.
  • Food follows the branchWorking from another branch moves lunch and snacks to that branch automatically. Branch and pickup is one option.

Cars, paths and routes · 6

Rules

Every number here is an admin setting on the Rules page.

Transport
  • Who shares the car's locationThe driver's phone if the driver uses the app. Otherwise the first rider in the car. If that phone goes quiet for 3 minutes, the other riders are asked to continue.
  • Several paths per carAdmins can give a car more than one path (for example via Mirpur Rd or via Rokeya Sarani, or an evening-only path). Riders choosing another car pick their pickup on any of its paths, but only within the radius set in Rules (default 500 m from where they'll be). Each trip's path is chosen from where that day's riders are.
  • Choosing how you travelHome branch: your car (default), another car, or on your own. Another branch: another car or on your own. Another car always means choose a car from the list, then a pickup point on its path.
  • Office detected automaticallyEach car's office comes from its route and where that day's riders are going. Nobody picks it.
  • Pickup zones and waiting timeEach pickup point has a radius (100 m). While the car is inside it and the rider isn't on board, waiting time is counted. After 5 minutes the stop can become a no-show.
  • Three ways to confirm boardingA tap on "I'm in the car", the driver or sharing rider, or location: within 30 m of the car while both move 150 m together. A push lets people undo a wrong match.
  • Skip a ride lateAllowed until the car enters the rider's pickup zone. The driver and the sharing rider are told at once.
  • Pickup point for a dayA pin on or near the route, for one day or as the new usual point. The stop order updates.
  • Ride another carAny car going to the right office, passing within 500 m, with a free seat. The pin snaps to the route. The host car and the usual car are both told; the guest shows with a Guest tag.
  • Auto route and mileageRoutes are rebuilt if someone skips or joins before the trip. GPS tracks are snapped to roads; km per trip and per day feed the mileage report.
  • Phone numbersEveryone on a car sees the driver's and each member's number, with a call button. Nobody else does.
Planning and food
  • Work from another branchAnyone, any day. Meals move to that branch's count. They pick a car that goes there and where to join it, or go on their own.
  • Plan up to 30 daysMark many days as coming or not coming. Coming days get lunch, snacks and the assigned car, in and out. Earlier one-day changes are kept.
  • Two deadlines, set in RulesFood confirmation is on: the admin sets it as hours before the meal or a fixed time the day before, per branch. Ride confirmation is off by default, so rides change until the car reaches you; it can be switched on as hours before pickup. Branch for a day follows the food deadline, because food always moves with the branch.
  • No requests, no approvalsNobody asks an admin for anything. Before the cut-off people change their own plan; at the cut-off the food counts lock; ordering from the vendor stays manual for now. After that, leave, "not coming" and skipped rides release the car seat and rebuild the route by themselves.
  • Leave always winsThe leave API is checked nightly and before each cut-off. Approved leave makes the day "not coming", even if it was planned. If leave is cancelled before the cut-off, the plan comes back.
  • Weekends and holidaysWeekends are set per branch. Holidays sync daily from the holidays app; admins can override any date or add a special working day.
  • After the cut-offFood is final and nobody approves changes. Meals already counted stay counted; the car seat is still released automatically.
  • ForecastPlanned days give the vendor a weekly estimate while daily orders stay exact.
Traffic
  • Found three waysGoogle's live traffic on every route, the car's own speed (under 5 km/h for 3 minutes away from a stop), and one-tap reports from anyone.
  • Confirmed by othersReports stay while people tap "Still there" and end after two "Cleared" taps or 30 minutes.
  • Who is toldPeople still waiting get a new pickup time when the delay grows by 5 minutes or more. Cars heading into a jam are warned. Admins see jams on the live map and delay minutes in the trip log.

Edge cases

  • No car goes to that branchThe app says so before saving; the person chooses to go on their own.
  • Sick in the morningFood is locked, so only transport changes. "Not coming today" takes them off the car.
  • Rider not at the stopWaiting time runs and the driver can call. After the max wait the stop becomes a no-show.
  • Wrong automatic boardingThe 150 m rule prevents most of it; a push lets the person undo.
  • Car breaks downAnyone in the car reports it. Admin marks it out of service and riders are told; they can choose another car or go on their own.
  • More riders than seatsExtra riders are moved automatically to the nearest car with a free seat going the same way; everyone affected gets a push.
  • Sharing phone diesThe last position stays on the map marked as old, and the other riders are asked to share.
  • Office opens on a holidayAdmin adds a special working day; the default for that day is "not coming" until people opt in.
  • Leave API is downAdmins see an error with a reference, and counts are marked as possibly including people on leave.
  • No smartphoneThe web version works on an office computer, or a branch admin sets the plan for them.
  • No internetThe app shows what was saved last and sends changes when it's back online.
  • New joiner or leaverThe nightly HR sync adds or deactivates them; admin assigns a car and branch.
